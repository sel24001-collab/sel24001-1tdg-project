What Is Acting?

Acting is about using our imaginations, using our voices and moving our bodies to create stories!
In Theatre Sports Club, we dont memories scripts, instead we play fun games to learn about the elements of acting.

Creativity is about using your imagination to create
funny characters, unique stories and interesting ideas.
We play a fun game called Bus Stop, were we create
over-the-top characters and stories to get the other
person of the bench.

Photo by Theo Crazzolara on Unsplash

Problem Solving is about thinking quickly,
fixing challanges that you face on stage and
create them into a new ideas. In the game Space Jump,
actors have to quickly come up with new ideas that
everyone instantly adapts to.

Teamwork is about wokring with each other,
listening to one another, sharing the stage
and developing fun stories together. In the game
Change, actor have to create a new line that is
different from the last when, when the judge
says "Change!"
